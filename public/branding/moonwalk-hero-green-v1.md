## Final prompt

Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: production-ready panoramic website hero background for Moonwalk, a New Zealand marketplace selling BOTH everyday cars and campervans.
Primary request: a distinctive, beautifully art-directed banner in Moonwalk's original vivid mint green #14BC7D, NOT pale pastel green #CAEBC5. Wide landscape canvas, approximately 3:1 ratio (2400x800 if possible). This is a finished background image, not a screenshot, browser mockup, or poster.

Scene/backdrop: a New Zealand coastal escape interpreted as sophisticated editorial travel artwork. A flowing pale coastal road hugs sculptural rolling green headlands above a calm green sea. Its broad curve subtly echoes a crescent moon, without drawing an actual logo. A small realistic cream campervan at the far lower right and a small charcoal everyday hatchback toward the far lower left give equal cues for campers and cars. They are understated details in the landscape, not dealership product shots. Keep both vehicles plausible, wheels grounded, no readable badges or plates.
Style/medium: a premium photographic/editorial collage with natural tactile grain, softly sculptural landforms, nuanced light and deep colour, restrained contemporary brand art direction. Elegant, inviting, fresh and memorable; neither cartoon, low-poly, toy-like 3D, generic tech startup gradient, nor stock dealership advertising.
Composition/framing: panoramic full bleed. Keep the whole central 50% of the width and the upper two-thirds quiet, dominated by a sophisticated deep emerald/mint atmospheric sea/background, with no focal objects, no sharp textures, and enough dark-mid-tone contrast for a white heading and translucent search field to be placed there by the website later. Coastal land, the crescent road, and small vehicles sit mainly in the lowest third and outer edges. Make the coast and sea themselves visible, do not fill the image with clouds or sky. A strong composition that also survives a shallow horizontal banner crop. Background remains visually intentional if cropped centrally on a phone.
Color palette: original brand mint #14BC7D as the unmistakable hero hue and edge light, supporting deep forest #101815 and #075C43, restrained warm cream #F3F7F1 for the road/van; nuanced green tones, no blue, purple, pink, or neon cyan takeover.
Lighting/mood: beautiful soft directional coastal light, subtle mint rim lighting, gentle atmospheric depth, tactile detail around the edges and calm darkness at the centre, grounded and editorial rather than sci-fi.
Text: NONE. No Moonwalk lettering, no headline, no search box, no typography, no watermark, no UI, no borders, no invented logo, no generic marketing badges. Fully opaque background.
